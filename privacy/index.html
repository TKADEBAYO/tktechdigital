01

Introduction

At TK Tech, we respect your privacy and take the handling of personal information seriously. This policy explains the types of information we may collect when you interact with our website or contact our business, why we may use that information and the choices available to you.

This policy applies to personal information provided through the TK Tech website, by email, through our contact form or through other direct communications with us.

02

Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with TK Tech. This may include:

  • Contact information such as your name, email address and telephone number when you submit an enquiry.
  • Business information you choose to provide when discussing a project or service.
  • Correspondence contained within emails, contact-form submissions or other messages you send to TK Tech.
  • Technical or website information that may be collected through hosting, security, cookies or analytics technologies where these are used on the website.
03

How We Use Your Information

Personal information may be used where reasonably necessary to operate TK Tech and respond to people interested in our services.

  • Respond to enquiries and messages.
  • Discuss, prepare or deliver requested services.
  • Communicate with clients and prospective clients.
  • Operate, maintain and improve our website.
  • Protect our website and business from misuse, fraud or security threats.
  • Meet applicable legal, regulatory or administrative requirements.
04

Legal Basis for Processing

Where data protection law requires a lawful basis for processing personal information, the appropriate basis will depend on why the information is being used.

This may include processing that is necessary to respond to a request before entering into a contract, to perform an agreed contract, to comply with a legal obligation, or for legitimate business interests where those interests do not override your rights and freedoms.

Where consent is the appropriate basis, you may withdraw that consent where applicable.

05

Sharing Your Information

TK Tech does not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with trusted service providers where reasonably necessary to operate our website or deliver our services. This may include providers of website hosting, email, analytics, communications or other technology services.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, regulation, legal process or an appropriate public authority.

06

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

Our website should be accessed using HTTPS where enabled by our hosting environment, which helps protect information transmitted between your browser and the website.

No method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security.

07

Data Retention

We aim to keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including providing services, responding to enquiries, maintaining appropriate business records and meeting applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and why it is held.

08

Your Data Rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable data protection law, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information in appropriate circumstances.
  • Request restriction of certain processing.
  • Object to certain uses of your information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on your consent.

To make a privacy-related request, email us at tktech.business@outlook.com .

09

Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for website functionality, security or performance.

If analytics or other non-essential tracking technologies are used, they may collect information about how visitors interact with the website, such as pages visited, device or browser information and general usage patterns.

Where required, appropriate information and choices should be provided before non-essential cookies are placed on your device.

10

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or platforms, including social media services.

TK Tech does not control the privacy practices of those third parties. We recommend reviewing their privacy information before providing personal data through their services.

11

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, website practices or applicable requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed when appropriate.
